Talk to your computer.

kai is a calm voice assistant that helps you manage your computer. Ask, and it listens, looks and acts. In your language.

What kai can do

Real-time voice

Natural conversation with Gemini 3.8 Live. Interrupt it any time. It speaks more than 70 languages.

Knows your system

CPU, memory, disk and battery at a glance. "Why is my laptop so slow?" gets a real answer.

Acts for you

Opens apps, searches and browses the web in its own window, clicks, types and fills forms. Anything sensitive, like deleting or closing, asks you first.

Sees what it does

It reads web pages and app windows like a screen reader does, and takes a screenshot only when it needs to look. Nothing is captured in the background.

Your key, your machine

No kai servers in between. kai talks straight to Google with your own key, stored in your system keychain.

Out of your way

A slim line of light above your taskbar that glows when it listens and moves when it speaks. Click it to see the conversation.

Get your free API key

kai runs on Google's Gemini 3.8 Live model, which is free to use with your own key. It takes about a minute:

  1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and accept the terms.
  3. Copy the key and paste it into kai the first time you open it.

Your key is stored in your system's secure keychain and is only ever sent to Google. On the free tier Google may use your conversations to improve its products; enabling billing on your key opts you out. Free-tier usage limits are shown in AI Studio.

Download

Installers for Windows and Linux are on the way. Until then, run kai from source (Python 3.11+):

git clone https://github.com/ccarrillomanzanares/kai
cd kai
python -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate   # Windows: .venv\Scripts\activate
pip install -e .
kai

On Linux you also need PortAudio for audio (sudo apt install libportaudio2) and an X11 session. To let kai read app windows, install python3-gi gir1.2-atspi-2.0 and create the environment with python -m venv --system-site-packages .venv. macOS is not supported for now.
